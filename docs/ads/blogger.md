If you’ve seen the video about cutting a week’s work to 10 minutes with Claude Code and SKILLS, you know the promise: AI that learns and uses huge numbers of expert skills. You also know the problem: 100k+ skills and 120k+ MCP tools. That’s more than any human can configure—and loading everything into the AI hurts performance (the “paradox of choice”).

The answer isn’t more tools. It’s teaching the AI **how to find** the right one. That’s the idea behind **SP Search** and the **3-step protocol**: analyze the target → get access (API key, DB) → let the AI auto-build. Add Playwright stealth for structure analysis, official API access for production, and skills that the AI can discover and install on its own (e.g. Modulist). The result: you stop being the miner and start being the **conductor**—the “Productivity Singularity” playbook.

- **7 categories:** Scale & the human limit, SP Search, 3-step protocol, stealth & structure, autonomous skills, productivity singularity, and a concrete solo-founder checklist.
- **23 steps** in order, with no redundancy—each concept appears once, with explanations and research notes.
- **Video timestamps** so you can watch the reference video and read along.
- **One “full step list”** at the bottom so you can run the protocol as a single checklist.

The app is a simple Next.js site: open it, follow the categories, and use the tables to learn and execute. No signup, no paywall.
